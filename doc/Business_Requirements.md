# Business Requirements

#### Objective

Build an end-to-end Retail Analytics Platform that helps management make better business decisions.

#### Stakeholders

- CEO
- Sales Manager
- Marketing Team
- Inventory Manager
- Finance Team

#### Business Problems

1. Which products generate maximum profit?

2. Which customers are most valuable?

3. Which products should be stocked more?

4. Which cities perform best?

5. Which regions have declining sales?

6. How much inventory is needed next month?

7. Which products are frequently bought together?

8. Which customers are likely to churn?

#### Success Metrics

- Increase revenue

- Reduce inventory cost

- Improve customer retention

- Increase profit

- Improve demand forecasting
